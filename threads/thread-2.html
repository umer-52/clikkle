I'm trying to integrate Google OAuth2 inside my React application. I created the web app Client ID on Google Developer Console and pasted it into Clikkle Auth settings. However, during login, Google gives a redirect_uri_mismatch error. What am I doing wrong, and what should my redirect URI be?

TL;DR

Copy the redirect URI supplied in the Clikkle Auth provider configuration modal and paste it exactly into the Authorized Redirect URIs field of your Google Developer Console OAuth 2.0 Client credentials.

James_Clikklesupport
21 May, 2026, 14:40

Hey VibeCoder!

This error occurs when the URL your application asks Google to redirect back to does not match the list of allowed URIs in your Google Developer Console.

To fix this:

1. Open your Clikkle Console and navigate to your project's Auth page, then select the Settings tab.

2. Click on Google under OAuth2 Providers.

3. You will see a pre-generated, read-only field labeled Redirect URI (it looks like https://cloud.clikkle.com/v1/account/sessions/oauth2/callback/google/YOUR_PROJECT_ID). Copy this entire URI.

4. Go to the [Google Developer Console](https://console.cloud.google.com/).

5. Select your project, navigate to APIs & Services > Credentials.

6. Under OAuth 2.0 Client IDs, edit the Web Application credentials you created.

7. Scroll down to the Authorized redirect URIs section and click ADD URI.

8. Paste the exact callback URL you copied from the Clikkle console, and save the settings.

Note that it may take a minute or two for Google's servers to apply the updated redirect whitelist. Try signing in again after saving and let us know if it works!
